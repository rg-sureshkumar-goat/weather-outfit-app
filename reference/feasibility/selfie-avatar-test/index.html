What to check

  • Does either avatar read as you, in skin tone and hair?
  • Which looks better: the exact colors or the palette match?
  • Was the hair length right?
  • How long did the first load and the processing take on your phone?
  • Try a second photo in different light, such as daylight from a window. Do the colors stay close?

Not read from the photo

Hair texture (straight, wavy, curly), facial hair, glasses and eye color. These models don't detect them, so in the app they would be manual choices.

First-use download

In the app, about 16.5 MB, once: the model runtime (11.8 MB, shared with the garment cutout tool), the face model (3.8 MB) and the hair model (0.8 MB). This test page sends the two models as text, so it downloads about 18 MB.

Face and hair models: Google MediaPipe, Apache 2.0. Skin palette: Monk Skin Tone Scale by Ellis Monk, CC BY 4.0. Character: original artwork for this project.
