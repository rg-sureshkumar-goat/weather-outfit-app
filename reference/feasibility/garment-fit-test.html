What to judge

  • On the front, does the print look like the photo? Check that the ALOHA 26 label reads correctly and isn't mirrored.
  • Turn to Side. How much do the pattern and sleeves stretch where the photo wraps around?
  • Turn to Back. With the sample, FIT TEST 26 should read correctly from behind.
  • Raise the arms. Does the shirt stay on the body and move with it?
  • With your own photo, is lining it up quick enough to do for every garment you own?

Not covered by this test

  • Final avatar art. The mannequin is a stand-in built from simple shapes.
  • Each garment's real cut. Every shirt uses one tee template, so an oversized hoodie and a slim tee would share a shape.
  • AI background removal, pants, dresses, jackets, and bending at the elbows.
